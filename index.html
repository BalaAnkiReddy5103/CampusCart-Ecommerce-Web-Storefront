Frequently Asked Campus Questions

How does the 90-minute hostel delivery work? +

When you place an order during operating hours (8:00 AM - 10:00 PM), our student delivery coordinators pick the items directly from the SAC Room 104 campus hub. The order is delivered directly to your Hostel Reception Gate, Security Desk, or Canteen Locker within 90 minutes with live SMS alerts.

Are the Casio calculators approved for university semester exams? +

Yes. The Casio FX-991CW ClassWiz models are 100% non-programmable scientific calculators compliant with Anna University, VTU, JNTU, AKTU, Mumbai University, and national competitive exam guidelines.

Can I pay using UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm)? +

Yes. We support Instant UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) as well as Cash on Delivery at your hostel gate upon checking your parcel.
